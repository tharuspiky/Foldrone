Security

Public security statement · 21 September 2026

Our approach

Security is considered throughout the development and operation of Foldrone products. The measures appropriate to each service depend on its purpose, data flows, access model and deployment environment.

Responsible disclosure

If you believe you have identified a security issue affecting a Foldrone website or product, please contact Foldrone through the official contact channel and provide enough information for the issue to be understood and reproduced safely.

Product-specific information

Detailed implementation information, internal architecture and security controls are not published on the public landing page. Where appropriate, product-specific security documentation may be provided through the relevant service or to authorized parties.
